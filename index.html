Kyojin gallery

Three open models running on one mini PC, driven by the Hermes agent: each one writes, runs and tests its own page. Click any picture to open it live. The cloud originals are at the bottom.

Runs use an AMD Ryzen AI Max+ 395 mini PC, the Kyojin 1.4 engine and the published Yamz packs, with the server defaults. Every page is opened in a real browser. The time under a picture is the time to the finished page.
